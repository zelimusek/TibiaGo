Party Guide

Welcome to CYRK'S PARTY ZONE!

Great music, crazy games and two DJs are waiting inside. Keep it fun for everybody.

  1. Respect other partygoers and the DJs.
  2. Do not deliberately block entrances, the bouncer queue or minigame squares.
  3. Follow Różal and Pudzian's instructions. They may ask a question, request a dance or collect the entrance fee.
  4. Everybody standing on the dance floor when a game starts becomes a participant.
  5. Stay on the dance floor until you are eliminated. Eliminated players must watch from the audience.
  6. Do not spam items or visual effects to make the game unreadable for other players.
  7. Turn up the music, join the fun and party hard!

Floor Is Lava

After the countdown, dangerous lava and safe tiles appear. Move onto a safe tile before time runs out. Anyone left on lava is sent to the audience. The final survivor wins.

Bomberman — Elimination

Add the Bomberman Bomb spell to a hotkey, destroy crates and collect BOMB, RANGE, SPEED and SHIELD bonuses. Bombs block their SQM and can trigger each other. One hit eliminates you; the last survivor wins.

Bomberman — Mayhem

The chaotic score mode. Hits cause an instant protected respawn instead of elimination. Keep bombing until time expires; the player with the best score wins.

Laser Chairs

When the music stops, the club lasers draw one fewer square than there are players. Claim a square before time expires; one square protects one player. The player left without a square is eliminated.
